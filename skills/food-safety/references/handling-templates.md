# FoodBridge Handling Templates

Use these templates for concise dispatch handling notes.

## Refrigerated Prepared Meals

```text
Keep refrigerated until pickup. Recipient should confirm acceptance before the available-until time and maintain cold holding after pickup.
```

## Frozen Food

```text
Keep frozen until pickup. Recipient should confirm freezer capacity before dispatch.
```

## Packaged Shelf-Stable Food

```text
Confirm packaging is intact. Recipient should verify labels and storage requirements at handoff.
```

## Produce

```text
Keep produce clean and separated from prepared meals. Recipient should inspect quality at handoff.
```

## Bakery

```text
Keep items covered and protected during pickup. Recipient should confirm allergen labels when available.
```

## Needs Review

```text
Do not dispatch yet. Ask the donor for the missing preparation time, storage condition, packaging status, or other safety detail.
```
